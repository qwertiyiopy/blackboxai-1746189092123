Our Story

Padiwara was founded with a vision to transform agricultural waste into valuable resources, empowering farmers and artisans while promoting sustainability and environmental stewardship.

Our Vision & Mission

Vision: To create a sustainable future where agricultural byproducts are transformed into eco-friendly products that benefit communities and the planet.

Mission: To empower farmers and artisans through innovative supply mapping, crafting techniques, and community engagement that reduce waste and carbon footprints.

Sustainability Commitments

  • Supporting community education and sustainable development goals.
